The Muse Duo returns to the Off the Dock Chamber Festival for its 2026 season, appearing both as featured ensemble and collaborative artists. Guitarist Collin Holloway and pianist/composer Luke Benedict, co-founders of Off the Dock, will perform as The Muse Duo and join fellow artists for *Musical Landscapes*, the festival finale concert featuring new works inspired by the Finger Lakes imagery of painter and poet John D. Barrow. During their time at the festival, the duo will also record new repertoire as they continue developing their evolving body of work for guitar and piano.
