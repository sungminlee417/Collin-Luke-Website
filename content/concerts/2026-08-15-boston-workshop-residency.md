From August 11–17, The Muse Duo will be in Boston workshopping a new piece written for the ensemble by Boston-based composer David Leach, a member of the acclaimed violin and piano duo Room to Spare. Known for pairing the traditional with the trailblazing, The Muse Duo continues to expand the repertoire for guitar and piano through new collaborations, original works, and a bold, evolving sound world. The residency will conclude with a small concert presenting the work developed during this artistic period.
