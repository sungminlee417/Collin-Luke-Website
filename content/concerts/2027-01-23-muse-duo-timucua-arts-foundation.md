For their January 2027 concert at Timucua Arts Foundation, The Muse Duo presents a program rooted in the tradition of classical chamber music while pushing the art form forward through accessible, engaging new repertoire. The concert weaves established works for guitar and piano with original compositions and commissions by pianist-composer Luke Benedict, creating a vibrant dialogue between past and present. In Timucua’s intimate setting, this program invites listeners into a living, evolving chamber music experience that is both refined and immediately compelling.
